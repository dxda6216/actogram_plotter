Actogram Phase Analyzer

Draw peaks and troughs as an actogram, pick the ones you want with the mouse, and get their phase in LD, LL and DD. In LL and DD the period and phase come from a linear regression of event time on cycle number.

Clock time of the data

Enter every date and time as the local wall-clock time on that day (standard or daylight time). Daylight-saving changes are handled automatically.

Peaks and troughs (hours after 0 h)

One value per line; a pasted spreadsheet column works. Negative values are allowed.

Light/dark conditions

LD uses L, D and the period it begins with; LL is constant light, DD constant darkness. Each condition lasts until the next one starts.

Phase reference

LD: phase = ZT ÷ (L + D) × 360°, with ZT0 at light-on.
LL/DD: event time t (h after the reference) is fitted as t = b + τ·n, where n is the cycle number (cycle 0 begins at the reference). The slope τ is the free-running period; the phase is (b mod τ) ÷ τ × 360°.
