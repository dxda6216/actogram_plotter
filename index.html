Actogram Plotter

Peak and trough times (hours after time zero) – typed in or detected in time-series data – with light/dark conditions, drawn as an actogram.

Green areas – from your records. Enter these from your lab notebook and experiment records: the clock time of 0 h and its time zone, the peak and trough times, the sample placement and the light/dark schedule. A wrong value puts the data at the wrong time.

Blue areas – view settings. Change these freely: day 0, the period T, the rows, ticks, labels and other plot options only change how the actogram looks, never the data.

⚠ Enter every date/time as the LOCAL clock time, as shown on a wall clock on that day – standard or daylight time alike (e.g. CST in winter, CDT in summer). Don’t convert daylight time to standard time: the switch and the offset to UTC are taken into account automatically.

Default: US Central Time (America/Chicago, CST/CDT).

Peaks and troughs (hours after 0 h)

One row per time point: time and signal (e.g. bioluminescence), separated by tabs, spaces or commas. Time = hours after 0 h, or a date and time in ISO 8601 form (e.g. 2026-10-05T10:15:00-0500, 20261005T1015+0500; seconds, “-” and “:” optional; without an offset: the time zone of the data). Header lines are skipped.

Sinc Filter: windowed-sinc low-pass (Hamming window), applied forwards and backwards; default cutoff 48 h, order 101. Moving Average: centred over the trend window, default 24 h. Detrended = data − trend. Peaks and troughs are the maxima and minima of the line: the raw data (default) or the detrended data, after a centred moving average (default 7 points; 1 = none), at least the minimum spacing apart (default 12 h). Unequal intervals are first resampled onto a regular grid (gaps longer than 2.5 intervals stay empty).

One value per line. Paste a column from a spreadsheet. Negative values are allowed.

Clear data empties the two lists (or, with time-series data, the time series and its hand edits). Clear all also blanks the clock time of 0 h, the sample placement and every LD start date, and resets the cycles to LD 12:00 / 12:00 followed by LL.

Sample placement

Placed at

Date and time when the samples were put into the light/dark conditions (LL, DD or LD). It is marked with an orange asterisk in the actogram (0 h of the data: green asterisk). Leave it empty if not needed.

Light/dark conditions

LD uses L, D and the period it begins with; LL is constant light, DD constant darkness. Each condition lasts until the next one starts. Click L or D to pick the length with the mouse wheel. After an LD condition, the Starts at picker of the next one lists the ends of D (for LL, or LD with Light first) or of L (for DD, or LD with Dark first).

Phase analysis

Drag to
Applies to

Click a point in the actogram to select or deselect it. Drag a box to select many at once; hold Alt (Option) while dragging to do the opposite. In the double plot each point appears twice; either copy works.

LD: phase = ZT ÷ (L + D) × 360°, with ZT0 at light-on (circular mean of the selected points). LL/DD: the event time t (h after the 0° reference) is fitted as t = b + τ·n, where n is the cycle number (cycle 0 begins at the reference); the slope τ is the free-running period and the phase is (b mod τ) ÷ τ × 360°.

Phase results

Summary table

Phase (h) is the phase in hours within one cycle: for LD the mean ZT (hours after light-on), for LL/DD b mod τ (hours from the 0° reference to the fitted event). Phase (°) is the same as a fraction of the cycle: Phase (h) ÷ cycle length × 360°.
